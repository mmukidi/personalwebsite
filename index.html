      What the EMA chat covered

      EMA vs simple moving averageA simple average weights every price in the window equally. An EMA weights recent prices more and lets older ones fade, so it reacts faster but is more prone to false signals in noise.
      Your goalA short-term strategy on daily bars, holding about a week or two.
      Suggested setup: dual EMA crossoverFast EMA (9 or 12) against a slow EMA (21 or 26). Fast crossing above slow is the buy signal; crossing back below is the exit. A 9/21 daily crossover is the usual starting point to backtest.
      Main risk namedCrossovers work in trending markets and get chopped up in sideways ones, producing false signals.

      EMA reference

      EMA today = a × price + (1 − a) × EMA yesterdaySmoothing factor a = 2 ÷ (N + 1). That gives about 0.20 for a 9-period EMA and about 0.09 for a 21-period EMA.
